Projectile, with and without air

Throw something. Change the angle and speed, then see what the air does to it.

Red: no air. Blue: with air.

Try this

  1. Launch at 30° and at 60° with the same speed. They land in the same place. Why?
  2. Find the angle that throws the farthest. Is it still 45° with air resistance on?
  3. Double the speed. Does the range double? (It goes up four times without air.)
  4. Throw on the Moon. Same speed, same angle: how much farther?
the physics ✎

Without air, the horizontal velocity never changes and gravity only pulls down. The path is a parabola.

R = u² sin(2θ) / g, H = u² sin²θ / 2g, T = 2u sinθ / g

sin(2θ) is largest at 45°, and θ and 90° − θ give the same range. With air, a drag force proportional to v² acts against the motion. The path becomes lopsided, the ball comes down steeper than it went up, and the best angle drops below 45°.
