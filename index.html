About

Ajmal Ibn Mohammed Althaf is a physicist, researcher, educator, and technology entrepreneur from Kerala, India. His work sits at the intersection of quantum technologies, scientific research, research commercialization, technology entrepreneurship, and education.

He is a doctoral researcher in molecular quantum mechanics at the Kalasalingam Academy of Research and Education, where his research addresses decoherence and noise in quantum communication protocols. He holds an M.Sc. in Physics from the Vellore Institute of Technology and a B.Sc. in Physics from the University of Calicut.

He is the Founder and Scientific Lead of QuantumX, a quantum technology initiative that develops quantum education programs, research collaborations, and early-stage quantum ventures. He previously served as Chief Academic Officer at Steyp, a Talrop initiative, and has taught physics at the undergraduate level.

QuantumX

Quantum technologies, research, and education.

QuantumX is a quantum technology initiative that I founded and lead as Scientific Lead. It works across quantum education, research collaboration, and the development of early-stage quantum technology ventures.

QuantumX currently builds education programs and scientific training through QuantumX School, supports research partnerships in quantum communication and computing, and develops applied projects with academic and industry collaborators. Its longer-term vision is a venture studio for quantum technologies: identifying promising research, assembling teams, and building companies around defensible scientific work.
