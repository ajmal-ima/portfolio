About

I trained as a physicist at the University of Calicut and VIT, and I'm now a part-time PhD researcher at the Kalasalingam Academy of Research and Education, working on molecular quantum mechanics.

Most of my working life has been spent teaching. I've taught physics in colleges, led a physics department, and built school curricula in quantum computing. Since 2020 I've run small communities that teach quantum computing in Malayalam, because language shouldn't decide who gets to learn this.

In 2025 I co-founded QuantumX, where I'm CEO and scientific lead. I'm also a Qiskit Advocate with IBM Quantum and was part of the LIGO India science communicator programme.

Off the clock: testing Linux distros, basketball, and mentoring students who are figuring out what to do next. I speak Malayalam, English, Hindi and Arabic, with some Tamil and a little French.

Research

Now: PhD, molecular quantum mechanics

Kalasalingam Academy of Research and Education · part-time · since Dec 2022

Computational and spectroscopic studies of bioactive compounds, with a focus on molecules relevant to cancer research.

Before that

My M.Sc. thesis at VIT, Decoherence in Quantum Communication Protocols (2021), looked at how noise and entanglement affect bidirectional quantum communication between non-maximally entangled parties, using noise models and fidelity calculations.

QuantumX

So far, our courses have reached learners in 12 countries, our community has grown past 2,000 people, and we hosted IBM's Qiskit Fall Fest in India in 2026. I still teach there, mostly through the Quantum Access School.
